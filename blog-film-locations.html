Long before travellers came for the dunes and the kasbahs, film crews did. For more than sixty years, the south of Morocco has stood in for ancient Rome, Jerusalem, Egypt, Tibet and fantasy kingdoms. Walk through the earthen alleys of Ait Ben Haddou or past the giant sets outside Ouarzazate, and you will recognise scenes you have seen on screen without ever knowing they were filmed in Morocco.

And the story continues: the new Mummy film from Universal, with Brendan Fraser and Rachel Weisz back in their famous roles, started filming in September 2026, with part of the shoot planned in Morocco. This guide takes you through the film locations you can actually visit, what was shot where, and how to fit them into your trip.

Why Hollywood loves Morocco

  • Landscapes that look like anywhere. Deserts, rocky plains, snow-capped mountains, palm groves, gorges and the ocean, all within a few hours' drive.
  • Light. The south of Morocco has strong, clear light and many sunny days a year, which is perfect for filming.
  • Real ancient architecture. Fortified villages, kasbahs and medinas that need very little work to become a film set.
  • Experience. Decades of international productions mean local crews, extras, horses, camels and craftsmen who know exactly how a film set works.

Ouarzazate, the "Hollywood of Africa"

Ouarzazate sits just south of the High Atlas, on the road to the desert. It is often called the Hollywood of Africa, and it has earned the name.

Atlas Studios

Opened in 1983, Atlas Studios is often described as one of the largest film studios in the world by land area. Its huge outdoor sets, including Egyptian temples, a Tibetan monastery and ancient city walls, have been used again and again. Guided visits let you walk through the sets and hear which films they appeared in. It is one of the most fun stops on the road to the Sahara, for adults and children alike.

CLA Studios

Opened in 2004, CLA Studios is the other big studio of the city. Large productions have built sets here for epic historical films and series.

The Cinema Museum

Opened in 2007 in a former studio opposite the Kasbah Taourirt, the Cinema Museum displays sets, props, costumes and old film equipment from productions shot in the region. It is a short, easy visit in the centre of town.

Kasbah Taourirt

Not a film set but a real kasbah, once a residence of the powerful Glaoui family. It is one of the most beautiful buildings in Ouarzazate and a nice break from the studios.

Ait Ben Haddou, the star of the screen

About 30 kilometres from Ouarzazate, the fortified village (ksar) of Ait Ben Haddou rises on a hill above a river, all earthen walls and towers. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and probably the most filmed village in Morocco.

Its walls and alleys are closely associated with Gladiator (2000), and it is the place many fans visit to relive the arena scenes of Maximus's time as a slave in North Africa. Game of Thrones fans will recognise it as the city of Yunkai in season 3. It has also appeared in many other productions over the decades.

Tip

Climb to the granary at the top of the hill in the late afternoon. The view over the ksar, the river and the desert plain is the best of the whole day, and the light turns the walls golden.

The Mummy: from 1999 to the new film

The original Mummy (1999) used southern Morocco to recreate ancient Egypt and the desert of Hamunaptra. The Sahara dunes and the stone landscapes of the south gave the film its epic look.

In 2026, Universal began filming a new Mummy film that brings back Brendan Fraser and Rachel Weisz, with a release planned for October 2027. The production is based in the UK and, according to the news reports, also includes filming in Morocco. The exact locations have not been made public, so for now you can visit the landscapes that made the first film famous, and maybe recognise them on screen in 2027.

  • Marrakech: the medina, the palaces and the nearby desert of Agafay have appeared in many films, series and advertisements.

The easiest way: a full day from Marrakech over the High Atlas and the Tizi n'Tichka pass to Ait Ben Haddou and Ouarzazate, with time for a studio visit, and back in the evening. It is a long day, but very rewarding. See our day trip to Ouarzazate and Ait Ben Haddou.

For film lovers, we can build a private route around the locations: Marrakech, Ait Ben Haddou, Ouarzazate and its studios, the gorges and the dunes of Merzouga, and Essaouira on the coast. Tell us your dates on our custom tours page.

Practical tips

  • Best time: spring and autumn are ideal. Summer is very hot in Ouarzazate; winter is sunny but cold at night. Read the best time to visit Morocco and the Sahara.
  • Studios: visits are guided and an entrance fee is charged. Opening times can change when a production is filming, so it is worth checking on the day.
  • Ait Ben Haddou: wear comfortable shoes for the climb, and bring water and a hat.
  • Photos: the best light is early morning and late afternoon.

Frequently asked questions

Can you visit the film studios in Ouarzazate?

Yes. Atlas Studios and CLA Studios offer guided visits of their outdoor sets, and the Cinema Museum is open in the town centre. Opening hours can change during active filming.

Was Gladiator filmed at Ait Ben Haddou?

Ait Ben Haddou and the Ouarzazate region are closely associated with the North African scenes of Gladiator (2000), and large sets were built again in Ouarzazate for Gladiator II (2024).

Where was Game of Thrones filmed in Morocco?

In season 3, Ait Ben Haddou became the city of Yunkai, and the ramparts of Essaouira became Astapor.

Is the new Mummy film being shot in Morocco?

According to news reports, the new Mummy film started production in September 2026, based in the UK, with filming also planned in Morocco. The exact locations have not been announced.

How far is Ouarzazate from Marrakech?

Around 4 hours by road over the High Atlas, depending on stops and weather. Ait Ben Haddou is about 30 minutes before Ouarzazate.

Can I combine the film locations with the desert?

Yes, and it is the best way: Ait Ben Haddou and Ouarzazate are on the classic route from Marrakech to the Sahara.

Por qué Hollywood adora Marruecos

  • Paisajes que parecen de cualquier lugar. Desiertos, llanuras de piedra, montañas nevadas, palmerales, gargantas y el océano, todo a pocas horas en coche.

Ouarzazate, el "Hollywood de África"

Ouarzazate está justo al sur del Alto Atlas, en el camino hacia el desierto. A menudo la llaman el Hollywood de África, y se lo ha ganado.

Atlas Studios

Inaugurados en 1983, los Atlas Studios se describen a menudo como uno de los estudios de cine más grandes del mundo por superficie. Sus enormes decorados al aire libre, con templos egipcios, un monasterio tibetano y murallas de ciudades antiguas, se han usado una y otra vez. Las visitas guiadas te permiten recorrer los decorados y saber en qué películas aparecieron. Es una de las paradas más divertidas camino del Sáhara, para adultos y niños.

CLA Studios

Inaugurados en 2004, los CLA Studios son el otro gran estudio de la ciudad. Grandes producciones han construido aquí decorados para películas y series históricas épicas.

El Museo del Cine

Inaugurado en 2007 en un antiguo estudio frente a la Kasbah Taourirt, el Museo del Cine expone decorados, objetos, vestuario y equipos de rodaje antiguos de producciones filmadas en la región. Es una visita corta y fácil en el centro de la ciudad.

Kasbah Taourirt

No es un decorado sino una kasbah auténtica, antigua residencia de la poderosa familia Glaoui. Es uno de los edificios más bonitos de Ouarzazate y un buen descanso entre estudios.

Ait Ben Haddou, la estrella de la pantalla

A unos 30 kilómetros de Ouarzazate, el pueblo fortificado (ksar) de Ait Ben Haddou se alza sobre una colina junto a un río, todo murallas y torres de tierra. Es Patrimonio de la Humanidad por la UNESCO y probablemente el pueblo más filmado de Marruecos.

Sus murallas y callejuelas están muy ligadas a Gladiator (2000), y muchos fans vienen a revivir las escenas de la arena de Máximo como esclavo en el norte de África. Los fans de Juego de Tronos lo reconocerán como la ciudad de Yunkai en la temporada 3. También ha aparecido en muchas otras producciones a lo largo de las décadas.

Consejo

Sube al granero de lo alto de la colina al final de la tarde. La vista sobre el ksar, el río y la llanura es la mejor de todo el día, y la luz vuelve doradas las murallas.

Dove è stato girato il Trono di Spade in Marocco?

Nella stagione 3, Ait Ben Haddou è diventato la città di Yunkai e i bastioni di Essaouira sono diventati Astapor.

Il nuovo film della Mummia viene girato in Marocco?

Secondo le notizie, il nuovo film della Mummia è entrato in produzione a settembre 2026, con base nel Regno Unito e riprese previste anche in Marocco. I luoghi esatti non sono stati annunciati.

Quanto dista Ouarzazate da Marrakech?

Circa 4 ore di strada attraverso l'Alto Atlante, a seconda delle soste e del meteo. Ait Ben Haddou si trova circa 30 minuti prima di Ouarzazate.

Posso unire i luoghi dei film al deserto?

Sì, ed è il modo migliore: Ait Ben Haddou e Ouarzazate si trovano sul classico percorso da Marrakech al Sahara.
