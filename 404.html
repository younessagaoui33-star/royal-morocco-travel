Private tours across Morocco

Where Your Journey Begins

Every tour we run is private, with your own driver and a guide who speaks your language. The programmes last from 3 to 12 days, and they start in the city that suits your flight: Marrakech, Fes, Casablanca or Tangier.

From Marrakech, the tours run from 3 to 10 days. They go to the Sahara at Merzouga through Ait Ben Haddou and the Dades Valley, and some of them end in Fes.

The tours that start in Fes last 3 to 7 days. They combine the old medina with the Middle Atlas, the dunes of Merzouga and the road to Marrakech.

The tours from Casablanca last 7 to 12 days. They join the imperial cities with Chefchaouen, Volubilis, the Sahara and Ait Ben Haddou.

The tours from Tangier last 6 to 12 days. They begin in the north, with Chefchaouen and the Rif Mountains, then continue to Fes, the Sahara and Marrakech.

Any of them can be changed: more days, other cities, other hotels.
