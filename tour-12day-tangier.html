12 Days Absolute Morocco Tour from Tangier

A full circuit from Tangier: Chefchaouen, the imperial cities, two nights in the Sahara, the south, Marrakech and Casablanca.

Overview

Embark on an unforgettable journey across Morocco with this 12-day absolute tour from Tangier, a blend of history, culture and landscapes. From the blue streets of Chefchaouen to the golden dunes of the Sahara Desert and the medinas of Fes and Marrakech, this itinerary offers a comprehensive exploration of Morocco’s most iconic destinations.

Tour highlights

  • Explore the cities of Tangier, Chefchaouen, Fes, Marrakech and Casablanca
  • Immerse yourself in the culture and history of ancient sites, including Volubilis, Ait Benhaddou and the imperial cities
  • The Sahara Desert, with a camel trek and a luxury desert camp stay
  • Landscapes from the Rif and Atlas Mountains to the Dades Valley and Todra Gorge

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrival in TangierBegin your Moroccan adventure in the coastal city of Tangier, where the Atlantic Ocean meets the Mediterranean Sea. After your arrival, enjoy a guided tour of the Kasbah Museum, which covers Tangier’s history; the Hercules Caves, with rock formations and ocean views; and Cape Spartel, a viewpoint where the two seas converge. Overnight in a traditional riad in Tangier.
  • Day 2: Tangier to ChefchaouenTravel to Chefchaouen, a town in the Rif Mountains known for its blue-painted streets and relaxed atmosphere. Wander through the medina, visit the Plaza Uta el-Hammam and the historic Kasbah, and enjoy views from the hilltop Spanish Mosque. Spend the night in a riad.
  • Day 3: Chefchaouen to Fes via Volubilis and MeknesOn the way to Fes, visit Volubilis, Morocco’s best-preserved Roman ruins, adorned with mosaics, and Meknes, a historic imperial city with the Bab Mansour Gate and the Sahrij Swani Basin. Arrive in Fes for dinner and overnight in a traditional riad.
  • Day 4: Guided tour of FesSpend the day in the cultural and spiritual heart of Morocco: Fes el Bali, a UNESCO-listed medina of labyrinthine streets; Al-Qarawiyyin University, the world’s oldest functioning university; the Chouara Tannery, to see traditional leather-making techniques; and the Bou Inania Madrasa, an example of Moroccan craftsmanship. Traditional dinner and overnight in Fes.
  • Day 5: Fes to Merzouga via Ifrane and MideltTravel through the Middle Atlas Mountains, passing Ifrane, often called “Morocco’s Switzerland” for its European-style charm, and Azrou, home to ancient cedar forests and Barbary monkeys. Arrive in Merzouga and begin your camel trek into the desert. Dinner and a night in a luxury Sahara camp under the stars.
  • Day 6: Merzouga and the Sahara DesertSpend the day in the Sahara. Watch the sunrise over the dunes, visit a local nomad family to learn about their way of life, and explore Khamlia Village, famous for its Gnawa music. Enjoy another night in the desert, among the Erg Chebbi dunes.
  • Day 7: Merzouga to Dades Valley via Todra GorgeAfter breakfast, travel to the Todra Gorge, a canyon with towering cliffs that attract climbers and nature lovers. Continue through the Dades Valley, also known as the “Valley of a Thousand Kasbahs,” to see its rock formations. Spend the night in a hotel overlooking the valley.
  • Day 8: Dades Valley to Ouarzazate via Ait BenhaddouJourney to Ouarzazate, the “Hollywood of Morocco,” home to the Atlas Film Studios, where many Hollywood blockbusters were filmed, and to Ait Benhaddou, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and ksar (fortified village). Spend the evening exploring the history of Ouarzazate before an overnight stay.
  • Day 9: Ouarzazate to Marrakech via the High Atlas MountainsCross the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n’Tichka Pass, with views and stops at Berber villages along the way. Arrive in Marrakech, where the medina awaits. Overnight in a riad in the heart of the city.
  • Day 10: Guided tour of MarrakechSpend the day in Marrakech’s history and culture: the Koutoubia Mosque, the largest mosque in Marrakech; the Bahia Palace, a masterpiece of Moroccan architecture; the Majorelle Garden, a botanical garden created by Jacques Majorelle; and Jemaa el-Fnaa Square, a hub of street performers, food stalls and artisans. Farewell dinner in a traditional Moroccan restaurant.
  • Day 11: Marrakech to CasablancaTravel to Casablanca, the largest city in Morocco. Explore its modern and historical attractions: the Hassan II Mosque, one of the largest mosques in the world, and the Corniche Boulevard, a promenade along the Atlantic Ocean. Spend the night in Casablanca.
  • Day 12: Departure from CasablancaAfter breakfast, transfer to the airport for your departure.
