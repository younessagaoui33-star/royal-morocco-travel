7 Days Tour in Morocco From Fes

From the medina of Fes to the Sahara, Marrakech and Casablanca in seven days.

Overview

Discover the beauty and culture of Morocco on this 7-day tour from Fes. The journey crosses historic cities, dramatic landscapes and the Sahara Desert. Each day brings something different, from the old medinas to camel treks across the golden dunes. It suits travelers who want to experience Morocco’s charm and traditions.

Tour highlights

  • Explore the imperial cities of Fes and Marrakech
  • See the Middle Atlas Mountains and the Ziz Valley
  • Ride a camel and spend a night in a luxury desert camp in the Sahara
  • Visit landmarks such as Ait Benhaddou and Todra Gorge
  • Discover Morocco’s history, culture and landscapes

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrival in FesYour adventure begins in Fes, one of Morocco’s most treasured imperial cities. You are greeted on arrival and taken to your riad or hotel in the heart of the medina. After settling in, relax or explore the medina at your own pace. Fes is a UNESCO World Heritage Site with a rich history, culture and architecture.
  • Day 2: Full-day exploration of FesSpend the day in the streets of Fes el-Bali, the oldest part of the city, with its medieval architecture and souks. The highlights are Al-Qarawiyyin University, the oldest continuously operating university in the world; the Chouara Tannery, where artisans dye leather with ancient techniques; and the Bou Inania Madrasa, a 14th-century school with fine Islamic architecture. Taste traditional Moroccan food and meet the artisans in the bazaars.
  • Day 3: Fes to Merzouga via Ifrane and Ziz ValleyLeave Fes and travel through the Middle Atlas. Visit Ifrane, the Switzerland of Morocco for its European-style architecture and cool climate, and the cedar forest of Azrou, where you meet friendly Barbary macaques. Pass through the Ziz Valley and its date palm groves, then arrive in Merzouga, the gateway to the Sahara. In the evening, ride camels into the Erg Chebbi dunes and watch the sunset before arriving at a luxury desert camp, with a Moroccan dinner under the stars.
  • Day 4: Merzouga to Dades Valley via Todra GorgeWake early for the sunrise over the dunes, then breakfast at the camp and return to Merzouga by camel or 4x4. Travel to Todra Gorge, known for its tall cliffs, and walk along the riverbed. Continue to the Dades Valley, the Valley of a Thousand Kasbahs. Dinner and overnight in a guesthouse or riad.
  • Day 5: Dades Valley to Marrakech via Ait BenhaddouStop at Ait Benhaddou, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a well-preserved ksar, and walk through its old alleys. Cross the High Atlas over the Tizi n’Tichka Pass, with views of peaks, terraced fields and Berber villages. Arrive in Marrakech in the evening and check into your riad or hotel.
  • Day 6: Full-day exploration of MarrakechSpend the day in Marrakech: Jemaa el-Fnaa, the central square with food stalls and entertainers; the Koutoubia Mosque and its minaret; the Bahia Palace; and the Majorelle Garden, created by the French artist Jacques Majorelle. In the evening, a Moroccan dinner at a local restaurant.
  • Day 7: Marrakech to CasablancaLeave Marrakech for Casablanca, the largest city in Morocco. Depending on your departure time, you can visit the Hassan II Mosque on the Atlantic coast and the Corniche, a waterfront area with cafes and restaurants. The tour ends with your transfer to the airport for your onward flight.
