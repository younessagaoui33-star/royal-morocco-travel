4 Days Morocco Desert Tour from Fes to Marrakech

The Atlas, the Sahara and the kasbahs of the south, from Fes to Marrakech in four days.

Overview

Embark on a 4-day desert tour from Fes to Marrakech, crossing the landscapes of the Atlas Mountains, the Sahara Desert and the culture of southern Morocco. The itinerary has natural wonders, historical landmarks and authentic Moroccan experiences that show the variety of the country.

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Fes to Merzouga via Ifrane, Azrou and Ziz ValleyLeave Fes early and head to the Middle Atlas, with views of cedar forests, mountains and villages. Stop in Ifrane, called the Switzerland of Morocco for its alpine architecture and greenery, and walk through the town. Continue to the cedar forests of Azrou, home to Barbary macaques, and pause to meet them. Lunch in Midelt, a small town between the Middle and High Atlas, then drive through the Ziz Valley, an oasis of palm groves and kasbahs, with stops for photos. Reach Merzouga, the gateway to the Sahara, in the late afternoon, where a camel caravan takes you across the dunes of Erg Chebbi at sunset. Overnight in a luxury desert camp with comfortable tents, a Moroccan dinner and Berber music under the stars.
  • Day 2: Merzouga to Dades Valley via Rissani and Todra GorgeWake up early for the sunrise over the Sahara. After breakfast, return to Merzouga by camel or 4x4. Visit Rissani, a historic town known for its souks and old trade routes, to explore the market and try local specialties such as medfouna (Berber pizza). Continue to Todra Gorge, where cliffs over 300 metres high form a canyon, and walk along the riverbed. End the day in the Dades Valley, the Valley of a Thousand Kasbahs, with its rock formations, including the Monkey Fingers, and Berber villages. Dinner and overnight in a riad or guesthouse.
  • Day 3: Dades Valley to Ouarzazate via Skoura and Ait BenhaddouDrive through the Skoura Oasis, with its palm groves and old kasbahs, and visit Kasbah Amridil, a well-preserved fortress that shows traditional Moroccan life. Visit Ait Benhaddou, a fortified village known for its architecture and history, and a filming location for movies like Gladiator and Game of Thrones. Arrive in Ouarzazate, a city of film studios and old kasbahs, and tour the Kasbah Taourirt. Overnight in Ouarzazate.
  • Day 4: Ouarzazate to Marrakech via the High Atlas MountainsCross the High Atlas over the Tizi n’Tichka Pass, with views of winding roads, peaks and Berber villages. Arrive in Marrakech in the late afternoon, where the tour ends, with time to explore the medina, the souks and the landmarks of the city.
