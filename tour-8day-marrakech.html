8 Days Essential Morocco Tour from Marrakech

Marrakech, the Atlas, the Sahara, the Draa Valley, Taroudant and the Atlantic coast at Essaouira.

Overview

Embark on an 8-day journey through Morocco, starting from Marrakech and exploring its landscapes and cities. From the Atlas Mountains and ancient kasbahs to the golden dunes of the Sahara, this tour gives an essential view of Morocco’s culture, history and natural beauty.

Tour highlights

Cultural immersionAncient kasbahs, Berber villages and the medinas of Marrakech and Essaouira.
Natural wondersThe High Atlas Mountains, the Sahara Desert and the Atlantic coastline.
Authentic adventuresCamel trekking, oases and a night in a luxury desert camp.
Gastronomic delightsTraditional Moroccan dishes, from tagines to fresh seafood.

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrival in MarrakechYour guide welcomes you in Marrakech and takes you to your hotel or riad. In the evening, explore Jemaa el-Fnaa Square, the heart of Marrakech, with its storytellers, musicians, snake charmers and food vendors. Overnight in a traditional riad.
  • Day 2: Marrakech to Ait Benhaddou via the High Atlas MountainsDepart Marrakech and cross the High Atlas over the Tizi n’Tichka Pass, past changing landscapes and Berber villages. Visit Ait Benhaddou, a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its earthen architecture and a filming location for Gladiator and Game of Thrones. Continue to Ouarzazate, the Hollywood of Africa, for the night in a riad.
  • Day 3: Ouarzazate to Dades ValleyStart with the Taourirt Kasbah in Ouarzazate, an example of traditional Moroccan architecture. Travel through the Valley of Roses, known for its rose gardens and rosewater, with stops in small Berber villages. End the day in the Dades Valley, known for its rock formations and greenery, in a riad overlooking the valley.
  • Day 4: Dades Valley to Merzouga DesertAfter breakfast, visit Todra Gorge, with limestone cliffs over 300 metres high, and walk along the riverbed. Travel to Merzouga, the gateway to the Sahara. At sunset, ride camels across the Erg Chebbi dunes to a luxury desert camp, with a traditional Moroccan dinner under the stars and a night in a Berber tent.
  • Day 5: Merzouga to Zagora via AlnifWake up early for the sunrise over the Sahara. Leave Merzouga and cross the Saghro Mountains, with a stop in Alnif, a town known for its fossils. Arrive in Zagora, a historic town on the old caravan route to Timbuktu, and see the Timbuktu sign.
  • Day 6: Zagora to Taroudant via the Draa ValleyTravel through the Draa Valley, home to Morocco’s largest palm grove, with kasbahs, old villages and oases. Reach Taroudant, called the Grandmother of Marrakech for its smaller but lively medina. Walk along its old walls and shop for handicrafts in the souks. Overnight in a riad.
  • Day 7: Taroudant to EssaouiraHead to the coastal town of Essaouira, known for its medina, art scene and beaches. Stroll through the medina, a UNESCO World Heritage Site of white buildings and blue doors, visit the fishing port, eat fresh seafood and watch the sunset over the Atlantic. Overnight in a seaside riad.
  • Day 8: Essaouira to MarrakechReturn to Marrakech through groves of argan trees, with a stop at a local women’s cooperative to learn how argan oil is made. In Marrakech, a farewell dinner at a traditional Moroccan restaurant.
