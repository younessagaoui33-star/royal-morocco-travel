3 Day Desert Tour from Marrakech

A private journey over the High Atlas to the Erg Chebbi dunes and back, with a night in a desert camp.

Overview

Experience the best of southern Morocco on our 3 Day Desert Tour from Marrakech, a private journey from Marrakech to the spectacular Sahara Desert and back. The tour takes you across the High Atlas Mountains, through the famous Tizi n’Tichka Pass, and onward to the UNESCO World Heritage Site of Ait Ben Haddou.

Continue through Ouarzazate, the Valley of Roses and the dramatic Dades Valley before reaching Merzouga and the Erg Chebbi dunes, the heart of Morocco’s Sahara Desert. Along the way, discover spectacular landscapes, traditional kasbahs and the impressive Todra Gorge.

The highlight is your Sahara experience. Ride camels through the golden Erg Chebbi dunes, watch the sunset over the desert, enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner with Berber music around the campfire, try sandboarding and spend the night at a Sahara desert camp beneath the stars.

On the final day, wake up early for the desert sunrise before beginning your journey back to Marrakech through the Draa Valley, Ouarzazate and the High Atlas Mountains.

It is a great choice for travelers with limited time who want to experience the Sahara from Marrakech, with a private and personalized journey and a local driver/guide.
