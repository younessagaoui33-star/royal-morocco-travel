Ouzoud Falls Day Trip

The tallest waterfall in North Africa, in the Atlas mountains, with trails, a boat ride and wild macaques.

Overview

A full-day trip to Ouzoud Falls, a natural wonder in the Atlas mountains known for its cascading waterfalls, green surroundings and calm atmosphere. The day mixes scenery, local culture and time outdoors, and it suits travellers looking for both peace and a little adventure.

About 3 hours each way by road.

Itinerary

  • Departure from MarrakechYour journey starts with an early morning pick-up from your accommodation in Marrakech. Enjoy a scenic drive of about 3 hours in a comfortable air-conditioned vehicle through the landscapes of the Atlas mountains. On the way, admire traditional Berber villages, terraced fields and olive groves, and stop for refreshments and views of the countryside.
  • Exploring Ouzoud FallsOn arrival you can choose easy to moderate hiking trails down to the base of the falls, with views of the water and the cliffs around. You can take a short boat trip to get close to the base of the falls and feel the mist. Look out for the Barbary macaques in the trees and on the cliffs, and bring some snacks for them.
  • Dine with a viewHave lunch at a local restaurant overlooking the falls, with traditional dishes such as tagine, slow-cooked stews full of flavour, and couscous with vegetables and spices. Finish with a fresh mint tea.
  • Relaxation and explorationAfter lunch you have free time to take photos of the falls from different viewpoints, walk past the small stalls selling handmade crafts and souvenirs, or simply enjoy the calm, away from the city.
  • Return to MarrakechAs the day winds down, you drive back to Marrakech while the sun sets over the Atlas mountains. You arrive in Marrakech by evening.
