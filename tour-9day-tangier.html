9 Days Morocco Desert Tour from Tangier

Tangier, the blue city of Chefchaouen, Fes, a night in the Sahara and the kasbahs of the south, ending in Marrakech.

Overview

Discover the heart of Morocco with this 9 Days Morocco Desert Tour from Tangier, a journey of cultural exploration, landscapes and unique experiences. The itinerary takes you through Tangier, Chefchaouen, Fes and Marrakech, as well as the Sahara Desert, with a mix of adventure and relaxation.

Tour highlights

  • Explore the blue streets of Chefchaouen, Morocco’s “Blue Pearl”
  • Discover the history and culture of Fes and Marrakech
  • A camel trek and an overnight stay in a Sahara Desert camp
  • Visit UNESCO World Heritage Sites, including Ait Benhaddou
  • The scenery of the Middle Atlas Mountains, Todra Gorge and Dades Valley

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrival in TangierYour journey begins in Tangier, a coastal city where European, African and Middle Eastern cultures blend. On arrival you meet your guide and see some of the highlights: the Kasbah Museum, full of history and artifacts; Cape Spartel, the meeting point of the Atlantic Ocean and the Mediterranean Sea; and the Hercules Caves, a natural wonder with views. Dinner and overnight in a traditional riad in Tangier.
  • Day 2: Tangier to ChefchaouenAfter breakfast, travel through the Rif Mountains to Chefchaouen, known as the “Blue Pearl” of Morocco. On arrival, stroll through the streets and photograph the blue-painted walls. Key attractions: the Kasbah, a historic fortress with views of the city; Ras El Ma Spring, a peaceful spot to relax in nature; and the local souks, with handmade goods and crafts. Overnight in a charming riad.
  • Day 3: Chefchaouen to FesAfter a leisurely breakfast, leave Chefchaouen and travel south to Fes, the spiritual and cultural capital of Morocco, passing through towns and scenic landscapes. Check into your riad in the heart of Fes and enjoy dinner.
  • Day 4: Guided tour of FesSpend the day in the ancient city of Fes, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The tour includes Fes el Bali, the oldest medina in Morocco, with over 9,000 alleyways; Al-Qarawiyyin University, the world’s oldest continuously operating educational institution; the Chouara Tannery, an insight into traditional leather-making; and the Royal Palace, known for its golden gates. Dinner and overnight at your riad.
  • Day 5: Fes to Merzouga via Ifrane and MideltTravel through the Middle Atlas Mountains, stopping in Ifrane, a town reminiscent of Switzerland, and Azrou, with cedar forests inhabited by Barbary monkeys. Continue to Erfoud, known for its fossil-rich lands, before reaching Merzouga, the gateway to the Sahara Desert. Take a camel trek across the dunes of Erg Chebbi to your luxury desert camp. Dinner under the stars with traditional Berber music.
  • Day 6: Merzouga to Dades Valley via Todra GorgeBegin the day with a desert sunrise. After breakfast, ride your camel back to Merzouga, then continue to the Todra Gorge, a canyon with towering cliffs. Drive along the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs to the Dades Valley, with Berber villages and rock formations. Night in a cozy hotel in the valley.
  • Day 7: Dades Valley to Ouarzazate via Ait BenhaddouTravel to Ouarzazate, Morocco’s film capital, and visit the Taourirt Kasbah. The city has been the backdrop for many Hollywood films. Explore Ait Benhaddou, a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its well-preserved kasbahs, a filming location for movies like Gladiator and Game of Thrones. Dinner and overnight in Ouarzazate.
  • Day 8: Ouarzazate to Marrakech via the High Atlas MountainsDrive through the Tizi n’Tichka Pass in the High Atlas Mountains, stopping at Berber villages and viewpoints. Arrive in Marrakech, the “Red City,” and settle into your riad. Spend the evening at Jemaa el-Fnaa Square, with food stalls, musicians and entertainers.
  • Day 9: Guided tour of MarrakechDedicate your final day to the treasures of Marrakech: the Koutoubia Mosque, the city’s largest mosque; the Bahia Palace, an example of Moroccan craftsmanship; the Majorelle Garden, a peaceful retreat designed by the French artist Jacques Majorelle; and the souks, with spices, textiles and handmade goods. Then a transfer to the airport or your next destination.
