6 Days in Morocco Tour from Tangier

The imperial cities, the Rif Mountains and the blue streets of Chefchaouen, round trip from Tangier.

Overview

Embark on a 6 Days in Morocco Tour from Tangier, an itinerary for exploring Morocco’s imperial cities, the Rif Mountains and the unique culture of the country. The tour blends historical discoveries, cultural immersion and scenic adventures.

Tour highlights

  • Discover the history of the imperial cities: Fes, Meknes and Rabat
  • Stroll through the streets of Chefchaouen, the Blue Pearl
  • Explore the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis
  • See the Hassan II Mosque in Casablanca
  • The landscapes of the Rif Mountains and the Moroccan countryside

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrival in Tangier and transfer to ChefchaouenBegin in Tangier, the gateway to Africa. Your guide meets you at the port, the airport or your hotel for a tour of the historic city: the Kasbah, a fortress overlooking the sea; the Grand Socco, the central square; and Cape Spartel, with views where the Mediterranean meets the Atlantic. In the afternoon, transfer to Chefchaouen, a quiet mountain town with a blue-washed medina. Walk its alleys and have dinner at a traditional riad, where you stay the night.
  • Day 2: Explore Chefchaouen and transfer to FesStart with a relaxed breakfast and continue through the streets of Chefchaouen. Visit the Kasbah Museum to learn about the history of the Rif region, and Ras El Ma Spring, with the sound of running water and views of the mountains. After lunch, drive through the countryside to Fes, the cultural and spiritual capital of Morocco. Dinner and overnight in your riad.
  • Day 3: Full-day guided tour of FesSpend the day in Fes el-Bali, the largest and oldest medina in the world and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The highlights are Al-Qarawiyyin University, the oldest continually operating university in the world; the Chouara Tannery, with its traditional leather dyeing; the Bou Inania Madrasa; and the Dar Batha Museum, with Moroccan art and artifacts. Finish at the gates of the Royal Palace and the view of the city from the Merenid Tombs. Return to your riad for the night.
  • Day 4: Fes to Meknes, Volubilis and RabatLeave Fes in the morning for Meknes, another imperial city, to see the Bab Mansour Gate and the Royal Granaries and Stables. Continue to Volubilis, the best-preserved Roman archaeological site in Morocco, and walk among the ruins and mosaics. Arrive in Rabat, the capital, in the evening, and see the Hassan Tower, the Mausoleum of Mohammed V and the Kasbah of the Udayas, with views of the Bou Regreg River. Dinner and overnight in Rabat.
  • Day 5: Rabat to Casablanca and transfer to TangierHead to Casablanca, the largest city in Morocco, for a short visit. Tour the Hassan II Mosque, one of the largest mosques in the world, and see its architecture. Then return to Tangier and spend the last evening enjoying the coast. Overnight in Tangier.
  • Day 6: Departure from TangierHave breakfast at your hotel, then transfer to the airport, the ferry port or your departure point of choice.
