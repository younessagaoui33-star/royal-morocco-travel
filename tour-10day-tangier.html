10 Days Holiday in Morocco from Tangier

From the Rif Mountains to the dunes of the Sahara, through Chefchaouen, Volubilis, Fes, the south and Marrakech.

Overview

Embark on an unforgettable 10-day holiday in Morocco from Tangier, a diverse and culturally rich country. From the landscapes of the Rif Mountains to the golden dunes of the Sahara Desert, and from historic cities to vibrant markets, this journey blends adventure, relaxation and cultural immersion.

Tour highlights

  • Explore the historical treasures of Tangier, Chefchaouen, Fes and Marrakech
  • The landscapes of the Middle Atlas Mountains, Todra Gorge and the Sahara Desert
  • A camel trek and an overnight stay in a luxury Sahara Desert camp
  • Visit UNESCO World Heritage Sites, including Volubilis and Ait Benhaddou

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrival in TangierYour adventure begins in the port city of Tangier, where Mediterranean and Atlantic cultures meet. On arrival, your guide greets you and takes you to the highlights: the Kasbah Museum, Tangier’s history and artifacts; Cape Spartel, a scenic spot where the two seas meet; and the Hercules Caves, a natural wonder steeped in mythology. Dinner and first night in a traditional riad.
  • Day 2: Tangier to ChefchaouenAfter breakfast, travel to Chefchaouen, the “Blue Pearl” nestled in the Rif Mountains. The drive has views of rolling hills and villages. In Chefchaouen, wander the winding blue-painted streets, visit Plaza Uta el-Hammam, the heart of the medina, and explore the historic Kasbah with views from its tower. Spend the night in a cozy riad.
  • Day 3: Chefchaouen to FesOn your way to Fes, stop at Volubilis, a UNESCO World Heritage Site with the best-preserved Roman ruins in North Africa. See the ancient mosaics, the arches and the sprawling layout. Continue to Fes, the spiritual and cultural capital of Morocco. Check into your riad in the medina and have a Moroccan dinner.
  • Day 4: Guided tour of FesSpend the day in Fes el Bali, the oldest part of the city and a UNESCO-listed site: Al-Qarawiyyin University, the oldest university in the world; the Bou Inania Madrasa, a masterpiece of Islamic architecture; the Chouara Tannery, to see traditional leather-making methods; and the Royal Palace, known for its golden gates. Traditional Moroccan dinner and overnight at your riad.
  • Day 5: Fes to Merzouga via Ifrane and MideltBegin your journey to the Sahara Desert through the Middle Atlas Mountains. Stop in Ifrane, known as “Little Switzerland” for its alpine-style architecture, and Azrou, famous for its cedar forests and Barbary monkeys. Arrive in Merzouga, where you trade your vehicle for camels and trek across the dunes of Erg Chebbi. Dinner and a night under the stars at a luxury desert camp.
  • Day 6: Merzouga to Dades Valley via Todra GorgeWake up early for a desert sunrise before returning to Merzouga by camel. Continue to the Todra Gorge, where towering cliffs create a dramatic natural canyon. Travel the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs to the Dades Valley, known for its rock formations and Berber villages. Spend the night in a kasbah-style hotel.
  • Day 7: Dades Valley to Ouarzazate via Ait BenhaddouStop in Ouarzazate, a city famous for its film studios and historical kasbahs. Visit the Taourirt Kasbah before heading to Ait Benhaddou, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and fortified village that has appeared in films like Gladiator and The Mummy. Dinner and overnight in Ouarzazate.
  • Day 8: Ouarzazate to Marrakech via the High Atlas MountainsTravel along the Tizi n’Tichka Pass, a route through the High Atlas Mountains dotted with Berber villages. Stop at viewpoints and learn about traditional life in the region. Arrive in Marrakech, where Jemaa el-Fnaa Square and the souks await. Check into your riad and spend the evening exploring.
  • Day 9: Guided tour of MarrakechDiscover the historical and cultural treasures of Marrakech: the Koutoubia Mosque, the largest mosque in Marrakech, with its minaret; the Bahia Palace, an example of Moroccan craftsmanship; the Majorelle Garden, an oasis designed by the French artist Jacques Majorelle; and the Medina and Souks, with spices, textiles and handcrafted goods. Farewell dinner.
  • Day 10: DepartureAfter breakfast, transfer to the airport in Marrakech or return to Tangier, depending on your travel plans.
