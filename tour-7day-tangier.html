7 Days Morocco Desert Tour from Tangier

The blue streets of Chefchaouen, imperial Fes, a camel trek to a luxury desert camp and the kasbahs of the south, ending in Marrakech.

Overview

Embark on an unforgettable journey with this 7 Days Morocco Desert Tour from Tangier, a blend of cultural exploration, stunning landscapes and desert adventure. The itinerary takes you from Tangier through the blue streets of Chefchaouen, the historic wonders of Fes, the Sahara Desert and the beauty of southern Morocco.

Tour highlights

  • Experience the Sahara Desert with a camel trek and a luxury camp stay
  • Explore Morocco’s imperial cities: Fes and Marrakech
  • Visit the blue streets of Chefchaouen, the “Blue Pearl” of Morocco
  • See the ancient kasbahs of Ait Benhaddou and the landscapes of the Dades Valley
  • Discover the cultural and architectural gems of Tangier, Fes and Marrakech

Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrival in Tangier and transfer to ChefchaouenYour adventure begins in Tangier, a city rich in history and cultural charm. Your guide greets you and gives a brief tour of the highlights: the Kasbah, a historic fortress overlooking the Mediterranean Sea; Cape Spartel, where the Atlantic Ocean and the Mediterranean Sea meet; and the Hercules Caves, a natural wonder with views. In the afternoon, drive through the Rif Mountains to Chefchaouen, famous for its blue-washed streets. Enjoy a relaxing evening in this serene town. Overnight in a traditional riad.
  • Day 2: Explore Chefchaouen and transfer to FesStart the day with a leisurely breakfast and explore more of Chefchaouen: the Kasbah Museum, with the history and culture of the Rif region, and Ras El Ma Spring, a tranquil spot with views of the mountains. After lunch, a scenic drive to Fes, the cultural and spiritual capital of Morocco. Check into your riad and have dinner in the heart of this historic city.
  • Day 3: Full-day guided tour of FesSpend a full day in Fes, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and home to the world’s oldest medina. Key attractions: Al-Qarawiyyin University, the oldest continually operating university in the world; the Chouara Tannery, to watch the traditional leather dyeing process; the Bou Inania Madrasa, a masterpiece of Moroccan architecture; and Fes El-Jdid, the newer part of the city, including the Royal Palace. Dinner and a restful night at your riad.
  • Day 4: Fes to the Sahara Desert via Midelt and ErfoudTravel south through the Middle Atlas Mountains, with a stop in Ifrane, often called the “Switzerland of Morocco.” Continue through the cedar forests of Azrou, where you may spot wild Barbary monkeys. Pass through Midelt, known for its apple orchards, and arrive in Erfoud, famous for its fossils. As you approach the Sahara, the landscape changes. Arrive in Merzouga, trade your vehicle for a camel and trek across the dunes of Erg Chebbi to your luxury desert camp. Traditional dinner under the stars, with Berber music.
  • Day 5: Merzouga to Dades Valley via Todra GorgeWake early for the Sahara sunrise. After breakfast, ride your camel back to Merzouga and begin the journey toward the Dades Valley. Stop at the Todra Gorge, a natural wonder with towering canyon walls, for a short hike or simply to enjoy the scenery. Continue through the Valley of a Thousand Kasbahs, a route lined with ancient fortresses and traditional Berber villages. Arrive at your hotel in the Dades Valley for dinner and an overnight stay.
  • Day 6: Dades Valley to Marrakech via Ouarzazate and Ait BenhaddouTravel to Ouarzazate, a city known for its film studios and nearby kasbahs. Visit the Taourirt Kasbah and learn about the city’s cinematic legacy. Then explore Ait Benhaddou, a fortified village and UNESCO World Heritage Site whose earthen buildings have been the backdrop for many films and TV shows. Drive through the Tizi n’Tichka Pass in the High Atlas Mountains, with views and photo stops. Arrive in Marrakech in the evening for dinner and an overnight stay.
  • Day 7: Guided tour of Marrakech and departureSpend your final day in Marrakech: Jemaa el-Fnaa, the main square, with entertainers, food stalls and artisans; the Koutoubia Mosque, the largest mosque in Marrakech; the Bahia Palace, an example of Moroccan design; and the Majorelle Garden, with exotic plants and vibrant colors. After the tour, transfer to the airport or your next destination.
